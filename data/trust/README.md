# Trust in UK government over time — data & figures

UK-first with EU-27 + US comparators. Generated by [`fetch_trust.py`](../../trust_data/fetch_trust.py); see [`trust_data/README.md`](../../trust_data/README.md) for full method notes.

**Coverage:** 1996–2025 · **3319 rows** · 29 countries.

## Data sources & citations

Every value is fetched from, or traceable to, the sources below — credited to the organisation that collected the data. Source observations are not fabricated by the tooling; WGI scores are source-published perceptions-based estimates, not objective measures of public trust. Each figure prints a source citation and the reporting-country coverage of any derived EU median/IQR.

- OECD (2026), with major processing by Our World in Data. “OECD average trust in governments” [dataset]. Original data: OECD, *How’s Life? Well-being Database* — survey item from the Gallup World Poll (“In this country, do you have confidence in national government, or not?”). https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/oecd-average-trust-in-governments
- World Bank (2026). “The Worldwide Governance Indicators: Methodology for Measuring Governance Using Perception Data.” Washington, DC: World Bank Group. Dataset: *Worldwide Governance Indicators (WGI)*, 2026 update, 1996–2025 (recalculated historical estimates), accessed 14 October 2026 via the World Bank API (DataBank source 3). https://www.worldbank.org/en/publication/worldwide-governance-indicators
